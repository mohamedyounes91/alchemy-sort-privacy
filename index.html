Alchemy Sort Privacy Policy

Publisher: Younestec
Effective date: 2026-10-24
Contact: Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com

The current version of Alchemy Sort works offline, does not require an account, and has no active advertisements, purchases, analytics SDK or cloud-save service. Game progress stays on your device.

1. Scope

This policy describes the ad-disabled 0.6.0 version of Alchemy Sort, its support contact and this privacy-policy website. The game contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK and requests no Android Internet, microphone or location permission.

2. Information stored by the game

The game stores completed and unlocked experiments, the active puzzle and Undo history, remaining Hint and Undo allowances, best pour counts, lesson progress, sound and reduced-motion preferences in app-private storage on your device. The application does not transmit this information to the publisher, an advertising provider or a cloud-save service. It does not require your name, email address, Google login or advertising identifier to play.

3. Advertising, accounts and purchases

The current version does not display advertisements or offer purchases. Prepared but disabled ad locations do not initialize an ad SDK, make advertising requests or grant advertising rewards. There is no in-game account or social/chat feature. If a future version adds advertising, accounts, analytics or cloud saves, we will update the relevant disclosures and implement any required choices or consent before activating those features.

4. Support email

If you choose to email support, we receive your sender address and the information you include. We use it to answer your request, investigate an issue or handle a privacy question, and retain it only as needed for those purposes or applicable obligations. Email is handled through our email provider. Please do not send passwords, private signing keys or unnecessary sensitive information. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

5. Operating-system, store and website services

Your operating system and app store may independently process installation, security or diagnostic information under their own policies. The publisher may receive store-provided reports; the game does not integrate an analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it in a browser sends the normal web request to the hosting provider, which may process technical information such as an IP address and browser details under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics scripts, tracking pixels or advertising cookies to this page. Opening an external link is separate from playing the game offline.

6. Retention, deletion and backup

Local game data remains on the device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to operating-system behavior. To remove it, use Android's application-storage settings or uninstall the game. There is no game account to delete and no publisher-operated cloud backup. Android app-data backup is disabled for this build. Uninstalling, clearing data, changing phones or installing a different application ID can remove or fail to transfer progress.

7. Children

Alchemy Sort is a general-audience puzzle with no account, chat or personal-information submission feature in the game. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through the application. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through support correspondence, contact us so we can address the request, including deletion where appropriate.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when relevant features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, contact Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com.
