Alchemy Sort Privacy Policy

Publisher: Younestec
Effective date: 2026-11-21
Contact: Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com
Account and data deletion: delete-account.html

The online version of Alchemy Sort creates an anonymous guest ID so you can appear in campaign rankings under a generated alias, lets you optionally sign in with Google to keep a cloud backup, and shows Google AdMob advertisements after you give or decline consent where required. You can delete your account and server data from inside the game or by emailing us.

1. Scope

This policy covers the online version of the Alchemy Sort Android game, the support contact and this website. It replaces the earlier offline-edition policy for versions that contain online features. Earlier offline editions (no Internet permission, accounts, advertising or cloud features) keep everything on your device and send nothing to us; their policy remains available on the offline edition page. The game still works without an account, and the campaign can be played offline, but online rankings, Coins prizes, cloud backup and ads need an Internet connection and the Android Internet permission.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores progress, the active puzzle and move history, best results, preferences, Daily puzzle history, local lives and helper gifts, a queue of wins waiting to be verified, and your guest session token in app-private storage. These are not encrypted by the game; they rely on Android's app sandbox. Android cloud backup of app data is disabled for the game. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your guest identity, and an unlinked guest account cannot be recovered afterwards. While you sign in with Google, the game briefly listens on 127.0.0.1 (your own device only) to receive the sign-in code from your browser. When you restore an existing Google account, the game keeps private local backups of your previous empty guest save for recovery.

If you choose Copy result, Save PNG card or Share result, the displayed result goes only to the clipboard, the location you choose or the app you choose. Those destinations have their own practices.

4. Third-party services

Supabase hosts our database and authentication (anonymous sign-in, Google sign-in, ranking and backup data) as our service provider. See the Supabase privacy policy. Google provides Google sign-in (see the Google Privacy Policy) and AdMob advertising (section 5). When you open this policy page, GitHub Pages receives a normal web request under the GitHub General Privacy Statement; this page has no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or advertising cookies. Your device's operating system and app store may separately process installation and diagnostic information.

5. Advertising and consent

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: two optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one to receive an extra Hint or extra Undo) and an interstitial ad that may appear after you finish a puzzle and deliberately continue to the next. Ads never start by themselves at startup or in the middle of a puzzle; rewarded videos play only when you choose them.

Where required, the game asks for consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before requesting ads, for example for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions. You can review or change your choice through the privacy options in the game's Settings when the consent form offers it. If you decline personalized ads, AdMob may still show non-personalized ads, which can use limited data such as IP address and device information. The game requests the Google advertising ID permission so AdMob can show ads; you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings, and apps then receive no usable ID. Google's use of data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Because ads are loaded from Google, the AdMob SDK is a third-party recipient of the data listed in section 2.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, ranking, Coins and cloud backup data are kept for as long as your account exists, because progress, ranks and prize receipts must stay consistent. We do not currently delete inactive accounts automatically. Deleting your account (section 8) removes them.
  • Support email is kept only as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.
  • Technical logs held by our providers follow their own retention periods.
  • Data on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the game.
  • Our backend provider may hold routine backups of the database for a limited period after deletion; deleted data is not restored to the live service.

7. Security

The game connects to our servers only over HTTPS, uses Supabase's public client key (never an administrative key), and the server derives your identity from your signed-in session instead of trusting values sent by the game. Raw database tables are not directly readable by the game; access is through restricted functions. No service is perfectly secure, and your device storage is protected by Android rather than by extra encryption in the game.

8. Your choices and deleting your account

In the game: open Settings, then Account, then Delete online account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and server data immediately: guest or Google identity and sessions, ranking scores and standings, prize claims, Coins and Coin Restart records, enrollment, verified attempts and cloud backup. The Google link is removed and your local guest session is cleared. Deletion cannot be undone.

Without the game: if you no longer have the game installed, follow the steps on our account and data deletion page or email Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com. We will respond and complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is not deleted: other players' data; ranking positions already awarded to other players in finished seasons (their rank numbers are not renumbered, so gaps can appear); our correspondence with you where we must keep it; data held independently by Google for your Google account or by AdMob under Google's own policies; and data on your device, which you remove by uninstalling or clearing app data. You can also choose not to sign in with Google, decline ad personalization, or reset your advertising ID without deleting your account. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information; contact us to exercise them.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support correspondence or an account, contact us so we can delete it.

Alchemy Sort is not directed to children.

10. International processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. We rely on the safeguards offered by those providers.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when features or data practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com.
