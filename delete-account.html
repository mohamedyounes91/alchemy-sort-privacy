Delete your Alchemy Sort account and data

Publisher: Younestec · Contact: Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com · Privacy policy

Alchemy Sort creates an anonymous guest account when the game connects for rankings, and you can optionally link a Google account. You can delete that account and its server data yourself inside the game, or ask us to delete it by email.

Option 1: delete inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open Alchemy Sort while connected to the Internet.
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Delete online account.
  3. Read the warning and confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

The game then clears your sign-in on that device. You can uninstall the game afterwards if you wish.

Option 2: request deletion by email (no app needed)

Use this if you have uninstalled the game or cannot open it.

  1. If you linked Google: email Mohamd.younes91@gmail.com from the Google email address you linked, with the subject "Alchemy Sort account deletion". We find your account by that address, may ask you a verification question, then delete it. We will not delete an account on the strength of an email from a different address.
  2. If you only played as a guest and have not linked Google: a guest account holds no name or email, so we cannot tell which account is yours from an email alone, and the guest sign-in is lost when the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared. If the game is still installed, use Option 1. Otherwise your guest data stays pseudonymous (a random ID and a generated alias such as Researcher-xxxxxxxxxxxx); email us your alias and we will review the request, but we may be unable to verify ownership and may decline in order to protect other players' accounts.

We will confirm receipt and complete verified requests within 30 days, usually sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your guest ID and, if linked, your Google identity as held by Supabase Auth (email and profile details), together with all sessions and sign-in tokens.
  • Your campaign progress record, Coins balance, Coin Restart and prize-claim records, enrollment, accepted-attempt records and receipts.
  • Your weekly, monthly and all-time scores and standings, so your alias disappears from leaderboards.
  • Your cloud backup (completed levels, best pour counts, lesson flags).
  • For in-game deletion, your sign-in and private backups on that device.

What is not deleted

  • Other players' data. Rank numbers already awarded to other players in finished seasons are not renumbered, so gaps can appear.
  • Coins and prizes are lost with the account; we cannot restore them.
  • Email correspondence with us, which we keep only as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations (you may ask us to delete it too).
  • Data held independently by Google about your Google account, and advertising data held by Google AdMob. Manage these in your Google account and in Android Settings (reset advertising ID, ad personalization).
  • Routine provider backups and logs that expire on their own schedule at Supabase; deleted data is not restored to the live service.
  • Progress saved on a device that is not connected: remove it by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data.

After deletion

If you open the game again, it may create a brand-new guest account with no history. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered, even by us.
